Weize Li

I am an AI Research Engineer on the founding team of a stealth-mode robotics startup, where I work on full-stack robot learning for manipulation, touching the whole system of the data collection, model/policy post-training, and inference deployment.

Previously, I was Research Engineer at TARS Robotics, and spent several years as a Research Assistant/Intern at AIR, Tsinghua University and HKUST, working with exceptional mentors to dive into 3D vision and graphics. And I am broadly interested in robotics, 3D vision, and multimodal learning, with a focus on foundation models for robotics, bimanual manipulation, and human-centric cross-embodiment transfer.
